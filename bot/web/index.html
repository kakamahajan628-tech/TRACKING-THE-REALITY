Observation policy

  • Live quotes: fresh for 120 seconds. WebSocket fallback begins after 60 seconds.
  • Daily statistics: closed candles only, with a two-hour release grace period after the next close.
  • Network metrics: source dates remain visible; a five-minute scan does not make daily data five-minute data.
  • Missing, restricted, insufficient-history and degenerate calculations are unavailable, never zero-filled.
  • Model and scenario outputs are separate from observations. No universal buy/sell score is calculated.
  • Every watchlist coin retains all 136 records, including unavailable metrics.
